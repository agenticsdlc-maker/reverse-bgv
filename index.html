Fair questions

Know what you’re getting.

Is this a background check on me?

No. Reverse BGV helps candidates research a prospective employer. We don’t verify your employment history or need your personal documents.

How is this different from reading AmbitionBox, Glassdoor or Reddit?

Those sources can be useful starting points. We organise available information, check recency and relevance, compare patterns, flag contradictions and connect findings to questions you can ask. Reverse BGV is not affiliated with those platforms.

Can you guarantee an unbiased report or tell me whether to join?

No research can promise perfect neutrality or predict your experience. Our approach is to show sources, include reassuring and concerning findings, label uncertainty and avoid unsupported verdicts. The final career decision remains yours.

Does this work for every industry and company?

You can request research across industries in India. Public information varies, especially for small companies, individual branches and specific teams. We check coverage first and tell you when evidence is too limited for a useful report.

Is the report completely AI-generated?

AI assists with discovery and organisation. Reports are reviewed before delivery. Anonymous reviews and AI summaries are not treated as verified facts; source quality and limitations remain visible.

When do I pay, and when will I receive the report?

There is no online checkout. We first confirm coverage, scope and delivery timing. We then share a ₹99 payment request and QR through WhatsApp or email. Please wait for that confirmation before paying. Tell us your deadline in the form; delivery is not instant or guaranteed by that date until confirmed.

Will you contact anyone at the company?

No. This service researches public sources without approaching employers, managers or colleagues. Your enquiry and individual report are not published or shared with employers.

What information do you collect, and how is it handled?

This static page does not automatically submit or store your form entries, use advertising trackers or upload documents. When you choose to send a prepared email or WhatsApp message, the information goes through that service to Reverse BGV. Your device or browser may retain autofill information, and the hosting provider may keep technical access logs.

We use the company, role, location, concerns and deadline to assess and prepare the report, and your contact details to respond and deliver it. Company research inputs may be processed by AI service providers; your contact details are kept separate. Please don’t include your name, current employer, identity documents, bank details or confidential company information in the research fields.

Email, WhatsApp and payment providers apply their own privacy practices. Your request and report are not published or disclosed to employers. To ask about retention or request deletion of enquiry details, reply through the same channel used to contact us; any records that must be retained will be explained.

What are the report’s limitations?

Public-source research may be incomplete, outdated or unrepresentative. Company-level findings may not describe a particular manager or team. Allegations are labelled as such; absence of negative evidence is not proof of a good workplace. The report is decision support, not legal advice, an employment guarantee or a formal certification of the employer.
